Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your construction journey with us.

How can I locate G K Builders in Tiruppalai, Madurai?

G K Builders is conveniently located near the E B Office, Ayyapan Nagar, Tiruppalai, Madurai. You can visit us during business hours or contact us for directions. We are also reachable via WhatsApp and phone for quick queries.

Where exactly is the company located?

Our office is located in Tiruppalai, Madurai, close to E B Office Near Ayyapan Nagar. This central location allows us to conveniently serve clients across Madurai and surrounding areas.

Do I need to make an advance payment?

Yes, like most professional builders, we do require an advance payment to begin your project. The advance amount varies based on project scope and scale. We recommend contacting us directly to discuss a transparent payment schedule suited to your project.

Does G K Builders inspect the site before construction?

Absolutely. Site inspection is an integral part of our process. Our technical team will inspect the site to prepare accurate designs, assess soil conditions, verify dimensions, and evaluate all critical factors before finalizing any construction plan. This ensures the safest and most efficient build.

Do builders & developers need a licence to perform their work?

Yes. Construction licences are mandatory. Prerequisites vary by state — builders must pass state licensure exams and register with the state board. G K Builders is fully licensed and compliant with all Tamil Nadu construction regulations, so you can build with complete confidence and legal assurance.
